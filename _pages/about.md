I am an undergraduate student in Optoelectronic Information Science and Engineering at the [Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)](https://sustech.edu.cn/en/), expected to graduate in June 2027. I work as an undergraduate research assistant in the Topological Physics Research Group led by Prof. Zhen Gao ([Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering](https://eee.sustech.edu.cn/en/), SUSTech), and I was a visiting research student with [Prof. Baile Zhang](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/blzhang/) at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, in Spring 2026.

My research centers on **artificial photonic structures and engineered wave systems** — photonic crystals, metamaterials, and metasurfaces — with the goal of controlling light propagation and engineering light–matter interactions. I am particularly interested in **topological phenomena** (quantum spin Hall / quantum anomalous Hall phases, valley and chiral edge transport), **nonreciprocity**, **synthetic gauge fields**, and **time-varying / spatiotemporal photonics**. Across my projects I have built a model-to-experiment workflow that connects tight-binding modeling, COMSOL full-wave simulation, projected-band analysis, finite-sample design, VNA-based transmission measurement, near-field mapping, and FFT-based dispersion reconstruction.

Going forward, I hope to extend this training toward integrated micro-/nanophotonic platforms and spatiotemporal photonic systems for **reconfigurable control of light**, with applications in optical communication, sensing, imaging, and information processing.
