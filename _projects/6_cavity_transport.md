**Context:** Collaborative research at SUSTech. The manuscript *Cavity-tunable Orbital Band Inversion and Helical Edge Transport* is in preparation.

## Research focus

This collaborative study examines how the cavity environment can modify photonic band structure and edge transport.

## My contribution

I contributed to the experimental work, including measurement troubleshooting and checks of the cavity and probe configuration. The experience strengthened my understanding of how experimental conditions affect the comparison between a photonic model and its measured response.

My role in this project was an experimental contribution. Manuscript authors are listed in their agreed order on the Publications page. Detailed result figures are reserved for publication.
