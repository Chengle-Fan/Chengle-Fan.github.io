**Context:** Undergraduate research with Prof. Zhen Gao, Artificial Microstructure Physics Research Group, SUSTech.

## Research focus

Synthetic gauge fields provide a way to explore how coupling patterns shape the spectra of photonic lattices. In this exploratory project, I studied connections between effective lattice models and full-wave photonic structures.

## My contribution

I developed tight-binding and full-wave models to explore gauge-field-induced duality and Hofstadter-type spectra. I also used MATLAB for spectral analysis and comparisons between effective-model predictions and electromagnetic simulations.

This work developed my modeling skills and helped me assess which features of an idealized Hamiltonian can be carried into a physical design. The project remains an exploratory study.
