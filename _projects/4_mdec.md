**Context:** Undergraduate research with Prof. Zhen Gao, Artificial Microstructure Physics Research Group, SUSTech. I am a co-first author of *Cavity-Tunable Dirac Phases in a Microwave Metasurface*.

## Research focus

The electromagnetic environment offers a way to tune a photonic structure's response. This work examines cavity-controlled photonic modes in a microwave metasurface and explores their potential for reconfigurable wave propagation.

## My contribution

I contributed to sublattice-resolved VNA measurements for the Dirac-phase study. These measurements supported the reconstruction of pseudospin textures and the characterization of winding numbers.

## Ongoing work

I am now investigating cavity-controlled refraction by analyzing simulated bands, isofrequency contours, and interface phase matching in MATLAB. This is an exploratory numerical study of candidate operating conditions for reconfigurable beam routing.

The Dirac-phase manuscript is in preparation; the refraction work is an ongoing exploratory extension.
