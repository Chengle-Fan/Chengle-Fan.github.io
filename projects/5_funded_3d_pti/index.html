Funded project · 2026–2027

Context: Project leader, SUSTech Undergraduate Research Training Program (June 2026 – June 2027). Competitive university research grant of CNY 10,000.

This funded project aims to design three-dimensional photonic topological insulators operating at telecommunication wavelengths (~1.55 μm), moving beyond the microwave and THz platforms of my previous work toward optical-frequency, chip-compatible topological photonics.

Planned directions include:

  • Designing 3D photonic-crystal architectures (e.g., woodpile- and scaffold-type geometries) that host topological surface states in the telecom band, using tight-binding-guided full-wave modeling.
  • Evaluating fabrication constraints of available micro-/nanofabrication routes (two-photon lithography, semiconductor process flows) and incorporating them into the design loop, building on my experience with fabrication-aware THz design.
  • Exploring how topology-induced robust transport could serve on-chip routing, waveguide crossings, and mode/pseudospin-multiplexed interconnects.

This page will be updated as the project progresses.
